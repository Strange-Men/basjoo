# SmartHome Product FAQ

## Warranty Policy

All SmartHome electronic products come with a **1-year limited warranty** covering manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use. The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidents, liquid exposure, power surges, or unauthorized modifications.

To file a warranty claim, you need:
- Proof of purchase (receipt or order confirmation)
- Product serial number
- Description of the issue

Warranty claims are typically processed within 5-7 business days.

### Extended Warranty

Customers can purchase an extended warranty within 30 days of the original purchase. The extended warranty adds an additional 1 or 2 years of coverage, matching the original warranty terms.

## Shipping & Delivery

### Domestic Shipping

- **Standard Shipping**: 3-5 business days (free for orders over $50)
- **Express Shipping**: 1-2 business days ($9.99 flat rate)
- **Same-Day Delivery**: Available in select metro areas for orders placed before 12:00 PM ($14.99)

### International Shipping

- International shipping takes 7-14 business days depending on the destination country
- Customs duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the customer
- Tracking numbers are provided for all international shipments

### Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number. You can also track your order by logging into your SmartHome account and visiting the "My Orders" section.

## SmartHome Hub Compatibility

All SmartHome devices are compatible with the SmartHome Hub (Model SH-Hub-3). Third-party hubs may work but are not officially supported. For the best experience, we recommend using the SmartHome app (available on iOS and Android).

## Battery & Charging

SmartHome portable devices use rechargeable lithium-ion batteries. Battery life varies by product:
- SmartHome Sensor: up to 12 months on a single charge
- SmartHome Camera: up to 6 months (with motion detection enabled)
- SmartHome Lock: up to 8 months

Charging is via USB-C. A full charge takes approximately 2-3 hours.
