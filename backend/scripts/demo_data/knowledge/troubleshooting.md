# SmartHome Troubleshooting Guide

## Device Won't Turn On

If your SmartHome device won't turn on, try these steps:
1. **Charge the device**: Connect the charger and wait at least 30 minutes. The battery may be completely drained.
2. **Force restart**: Press and hold the power button for 10 seconds.
3. **Try a different charging cable**: The cable may be damaged. Use an original SmartHome USB-C cable.
4. **Check the power source**: Try a different outlet or USB port.
5. If none of these work, the device may need a warranty repair. Contact support with your product serial number.

## Factory Reset

To reset your SmartHome device to factory settings:
1. Go to **Settings > System > Reset > Factory Reset**
2. Confirm the reset when prompted
3. The device will restart and return to its initial setup state

**Warning**: This will erase all data on the device, including saved Wi-Fi networks, paired devices, and user preferences. Make sure to back up important files before proceeding.

### When to Factory Reset
- Device is unresponsive or freezing frequently
- You're selling or giving away the device
- Persistent connectivity issues that other steps haven't resolved

## Wi-Fi Connection Issues

If your SmartHome device cannot connect to Wi-Fi:
1. **Check your router**: Ensure your Wi-Fi network is working (test with another device)
2. **Move closer**: Bring the device within 10 feet of the router during setup
3. **Check frequency**: SmartHome devices support 2.4GHz networks. 5GHz-only networks are not supported.
4. **Restart the device**: Power cycle the SmartHome device
5. **Forget and reconnect**: Go to Settings > Wi-Fi > Forget Network, then reconnect
6. **Restart the router**: Unplug your router for 30 seconds, then plug it back in

## Firmware Update Issues

If a firmware update fails:
1. Ensure the device has at least 50% battery
2. Connect to a stable Wi-Fi network
3. Do not turn off the device during the update
4. If the update stalls for more than 30 minutes, perform a force restart (hold power button for 10 seconds)
5. Retry the update from Settings > System > Firmware Update

## SmartHome App Not Detecting Device

If the SmartHome app cannot find your device:
1. Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone
2. Ensure the device is in pairing mode (LED blinking blue)
3. Close and reopen the SmartHome app
4. Clear the app cache (Settings > Apps > SmartHome > Clear Cache)
5. Ensure you're using the latest version of the SmartHome app

## Camera Image Quality Issues

If your SmartHome Camera image is blurry or dark:
1. **Clean the lens**: Wipe with a soft, dry cloth
2. **Check lighting**: Ensure adequate lighting or enable night vision mode
3. **Adjust resolution**: Go to Camera Settings > Image Quality > Select 1080p
4. **Check internet speed**: Slow upload speeds can reduce image quality
5. **Restart the camera**: Power cycle by unplugging for 10 seconds

## Lock Battery Replacement

When the SmartHome Lock battery is low (below 20%), you'll receive a notification. To replace:
1. Slide the battery cover off the interior panel
2. Remove the old batteries (4x AA)
3. Insert 4 new AA batteries (alkaline recommended)
4. Replace the cover
5. The lock will automatically recalibrate

**Emergency access**: If the lock battery dies completely, use the physical key backup or connect a USB-C power bank to the emergency power port on the bottom of the lock.
