# SmartHome Return & Refund Policy

## Standard Return Policy

You may return any unused SmartHome product within **7 days of delivery** for a full refund. The product must be:
- In its original packaging with all accessories included
- In unused, resalable condition
- Accompanied by the original receipt or order confirmation

### How to Initiate a Return

1. Contact our support team at support@smarthome-example.com or call 1-800-SMART-HOME
2. Provide your order number and reason for return
3. Receive a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number
4. Ship the product back using the prepaid shipping label provided
5. Refund is processed within 3-5 business days after we receive and inspect the item

### Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:
- Products with broken security seals
- Custom-configured devices
- Gift cards
- Products damaged by the customer

## Refund Policy

- Refunds are credited to the original payment method
- Processing time: 3-5 business days after item inspection
- Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error
- Express shipping fees are non-refundable

## Exchange Policy

If you wish to exchange a product for a different model or color:
- Contact support within **14 days** of delivery
- The exchanged item must be unused and in original packaging
- Price differences will be charged or refunded accordingly
- Exchanges are subject to product availability

## Defective Product Return

If your product arrives defective or develops a defect within 30 days:
- We offer free return shipping
- Full refund or replacement at your choice
- Contact support immediately with photos or video of the defect

## Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven't received your refund after 5 business days:
1. Check your bank account again
2. Contact your credit card company (processing may take additional time)
3. Contact your bank
4. If you've done all of the above and still haven't received your refund, contact our support team

## International Returns

International customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is due to our error. We recommend using a trackable shipping method. Customs duties paid on the original order are non-refundable by SmartHome (contact your local customs authority for potential refunds).
